Clipyard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Clipyard is a private tool used only by its owner, Leonidawastedclips, to upload the owner's own video clips to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

What data is used: Clipyard stores the login tokens that each platform issues when the owner connects an account, plus basic upload results such as post IDs. It does not collect data about any other people.

Where it is kept: All data stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared or sent to anyone except the platform being posted to.

Removing access: The owner can disconnect Clipyard at any time in each platform's settings and delete the stored tokens from their computer.
